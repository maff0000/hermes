# HMT-3 — Deferred Acquisition Families

**Initiative:** `HMT-3 — Post-HMT-2 HERMES Market Truth Roadmap`
**Status:** DOCUMENTATION / GOVERNANCE RECORD ONLY. Governs every HMT-3 item. See
[`hmt3-roadmap-overview.md`](hmt3-roadmap-overview.md) for the roadmap-wide non-authorisation statement.

## Purpose

Name, in full, the data families that this roadmap does not build or acquire, and state the evidence
bar that must be met before that could change.

## §1 — The list

- MBO/L3.
- Detailed cancellation/queue-position data.
- GC options IV/skew/OI.
- EBS spot-gold.
- Wider cross-market instruments.
- Rates/DXY/silver/miner structures.

## §2 — Do not build or acquire yet solely because they are interesting

> Do not build/acquire yet solely because they are interesting.

This applies to every item in §1 without exception. [HMT-3G](hmt3g-liquidity-resiliency.md) §3 already
states this specifically for MBO/L3 in the context of liquidity-resiliency facts — that MBO remains a
future evidence-gated acquisition decision — and this document states the same standard as the general
rule governing every other family in §1 as well.

## §3 — Future evidence must justify cost and architectural expansion

Before any family in §1 is acquired, future evidence must justify both its cost and the architectural
expansion required to support it. Interest, plausibility, or the fact that a family would make some
HMT-3 fact more precise is not, by itself, sufficient justification.

## §4 — Non-authorisation

This document states doctrine that governs acquisition scope for every HMT-3 item. It does not itself
authorise any implementation or acquisition. See
[`hmt3-roadmap-overview.md`](hmt3-roadmap-overview.md).
